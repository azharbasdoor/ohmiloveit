There are wounds that remain silent, tucked away amidst the clinking of spoons and gazes that offer nothing more than mere tolerance. This anthology is a journey—from the act of devotion to the courage required to step out through a door that was never truly wide open. Inspired by the melodies that accompanied nights filled with questions.

## SPECTRUM
*(Track 1)*

I once believed love was merely a matter of clear-cut black and white,  
Free from doubt or hues that harbor deceit,  
I placed my faith in promises exchanged through a meeting of eyes,  
Believing you were the final harbor for all my aspirations.

Yet, you turned out to be a shade of gray I could never decipher,  
A complex riddle that rendered my logic blind,  
You showed me a rainbow, only to snatch its light away,  
Leaving me behind in a darkness steeped in sorrow.

Now, I am trapped within the colors you crafted without love,  
Realizing that my own definition of happiness was never on the map,  
For even though I surrendered my entire life to your reign,  
Your world was never truly mine to hold or keep.

## SYMPHONY
*(Track 2)*

How much longer must we remain a hollow song,  
A mournful melody played on a loop, devoid of any love?  
I am weary of being mere notes you cast aside,  
Dancing upon a desolate stage, unseen by any welcoming gaze.

We are lyrics that have lost their meaning amidst our own despair,  
A fractured rhythm, born because you no longer care to follow the map,  
I strive to harmonize our voices, to maintain the illusion of a grand, regal existence,  
Yet your voice drifts further away, swallowed whole by the echoes.

I can no longer pretend within this symphony steeped in sorrow,  
Remaining a part of your music that leaves behind only old wounds;  
For no matter how long we sing this song together,  
The reality is that your world is not truly mine to inhabit.

## CEASEFIRE
*(Track 3)*

I am weary of battling an ego as unyielding as stone,  
Until all my forces have surrendered and retreated, one by one;  
My body, bruised and battered, lies shattered beyond repair—  
Coming to the realization that winning your heart is an uncertain endeavor.

## STRANGER
*(Track 4)*

In truth, I was never truly prepared to stand here,  
Watching you walk away, leaving behind everything we had carefully built,  
Yet I could only stand frozen, locked in an eternal silence,  
As your figure grew smaller beneath a sky that no longer felt serene.

I did not hold you back, nor did I beg you to stay by my side,  
For I knew that forcing you would only deepen the wounds within my heart,  
I let you go, taking with you all the parts of me that you despised,  
Toward a new world—one that, to you, perhaps feels more beautiful and meaningful.

Farewell to the love that once turned me into a dreamer,  
Now, I have become a stranger once more in a land I once held sacred,  
And as your silhouette vanished around a quiet, deserted bend in the road,  
I accepted the bitter reality: your world was never truly mine to possess.

## PIER
*(Track 5)*

Now I realize it was all in vain—an empty illusion,  
Placing all my hopes upon your shoulders,  
For the space I called "home" was merely a place for a guest,  
Left to grow cold behind a wooden door.

There is no longer any point in trying to win you over,  
Or waiting for a greeting that feels stiff and withered,  
For your heart is a vast, blue ocean,  
Yet I am not the pier you seek to reach.

I cast off the longing that held me captive,  
Ceasing to be a shadow forever left waiting,  
Even if it means shattering into ash,  
I refuse to remain trapped in your world any longer.

For I deserve a love that burns with passion,  
Not merely a silent, begrudging tolerance,  
So now, I shall walk away from you,  
Because your world was never truly mine to claim.
